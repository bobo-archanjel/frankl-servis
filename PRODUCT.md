## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static one-page site built with Vite (vanilla JS/CSS), Lenis smooth scroll + GSAP ScrollTrigger for parallax, self-hosted fonts, generated imagery and hero video. Chosen for speed, zero runtime framework, and static hosting.

## Users

Drivers in and around Nové Zámky (Slovakia) whose car needs service or repair, usually searching on a phone and wanting to call or get directions quickly. *(inferred from category and reviews)*

## Product Purpose

Frankl Servis is an independent car service (Google category: Automobilový servis) at Jozefa Murgaša 2793/70, Dolná kapsa, 940 64 Nové Zámky. The site's success is a visitor who calls 0908 321 615 or opens directions within seconds.

## Positioning

Per its own customers' words: reliable results, willing advice, fast help (a repair resolved the same day it was needed), and an owner who is personally involved. Rated 4,8/5 from 38 Google reviews.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Google Maps / search on mobile. Primary actions: tap-to-call, Google Maps directions. Language: Slovak.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Known: address, phone, category, rating 4,8/5 (38 reviews), closes at 15:30 (listing showed "Zatvorené od 15:30").
- Unknown and must not be invented: full weekly opening hours, opening time, specific services list, prices, brands serviced, founding year, staff names, awards, website URL, e-mail.
- Services copy may only describe the general category (servis, opravy, diagnostika, poradenstvo) *(inferred from category)*.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Frankl Servis". No existing logo, colors, or type known.

## Evidence on Hand

Three verbatim Google reviews (Filip Jenča, Robert Vágvölgyi, Pavol Sovič), all 5/5. No real photos of the premises; all imagery on the site is generated and must not be presented as documentary photos of the actual workshop.

## Product Principles

1. Contact first: phone and directions reachable from every viewport.
2. Say only what is true; customers' own words carry the claims.
3. Trust through craft: calm, precise, workshop-grade detail, not hype.
4. Fast on a mid-range phone on mobile data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard access, prefers-reduced-motion respected (no parallax, video paused to poster).
